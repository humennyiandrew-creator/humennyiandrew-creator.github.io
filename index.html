Clip Uploader

Clip Uploader is a private, internal tool used by a small clipping studio to publish short-form videos it has edited to the YouTube channels it owns and manages.

What it does

  • Uploads finished short videos (Shorts) to our own YouTube channels through the official YouTube Data API.
  • Reads basic statistics (view counts) of the videos we uploaded, to track their performance.

What it does not do

  • It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
  • It does not access, download or store other people's YouTube data, and it does not scrape YouTube.
  • It does not sell or share any data.
