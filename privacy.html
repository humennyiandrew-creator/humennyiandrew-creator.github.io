Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Clip Uploader ("the app") is an internal tool operated by its owner to upload videos to YouTube channels the owner manages. This policy explains what data the app uses.

Data the app accesses

  • Google account authorisation: when a channel owner signs in with Google, the app receives an OAuth token with the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly.
  • YouTube data: the channel's ID and title, and the view counts of videos the app uploaded.

How the data is used

  • To upload videos the owner has prepared to the owner's own channels.
  • To show the owner how those videos perform.

The app does not use the data for advertising, does not sell it and does not share it with third parties.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer and are sent only to Google's APIs. Nothing is stored on third-party servers.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local data removes all stored tokens and statistics.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
